## Current product priority

The current priority is the Qortium-native scheduled Auto-DJ radio station.

Core capabilities include:

- deterministic 24/7 radio timeline;
- default rotation;
- scheduled playlist programs;
- Week View and Agenda scheduling;
- immutable playlist versions;
- global audio engine;
- music library;
- Add QDN;
- listener likes;
- Request Show;
- station messaging;
- tips/donations;
- public station playlists.

## Explicitly out of scope

The future Q-Music-like community music platform is a separate future project.

Do not implement:

- general user music publishing;
- creator profiles;
- community playlists;
- broad music discovery;
- Q-Music-style social music platform

as part of the current NodeFM Station implementation.

The old Q-Music project may only be inspected later for visual or functional inspiration.

Its source code, data model and architecture must not be reused as the foundation of NodeFM Station.
